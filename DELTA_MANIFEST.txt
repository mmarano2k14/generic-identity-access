This increment adds durable local-session authentication assurance, exact-session TOTP/recovery/WebAuthn step-up, Required-MFA freshness enforcement for OIDC authorization, and auth_time/acr/amr propagation with assurance pinned across authorization-code and refresh-token grants.
PostgreSQL migration 0019_session_authentication_assurance.sql adds bounded assurance state to user_sessions, oidc_authorization_codes, and oidc_refresh_tokens.
Refresh-token rotation preserves the assurance pinned to the family; later local-session elevation does not silently elevate an existing refresh family.
Bearer validation requires the current local session to retain at least the signed token assurance.
